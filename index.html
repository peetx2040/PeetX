Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding platform operation, security standards, and decentralized smart contracts

What is PeetX and how does it work?
PeetX is a fully decentralized Web3 protocol operating on the Binance Smart Chain (BSC). Transactions and profit distributions are governed automatically by smart contracts without central servers or human intervention.
Are my funds secure on PeetX?
Yes. PeetX utilizes non-custodial interaction model. Your tokens stay directly inside your private wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, etc.) until peer-to-peer or liquidity transactions are initiated by you.
How do I start trading or participating?
Connect any Web3-compatible crypto wallet to our Swap Platform, ensure you have BNB for network gas fees, and interact directly with our verified BSC smart contracts.
